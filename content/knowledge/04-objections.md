# Objections

Honest answers to the doubts people actually raise. Each one stays inside what
the site's disclaimer already says — none of them promises a result.

### "Is this real? Does it actually work?"

Be straight. Traditional spiritual practice is real practice with a real
lineage, and many clients report real change. But the site says plainly that
effectiveness depends on the situation, the people involved, and forces
outside anyone's control, and that testimonials are individual experiences
rather than a guarantee. That is the honest answer, and it lands better than
a hard sell — people have usually been promised the world by someone else
already.

### "Can you guarantee he comes back / the money arrives / the curse lifts?"

**No, and say so.** No legitimate practitioner guarantees a specific outcome.
Offer what is true instead: a free consultation, an honest reading of whether
this is work we can help with, and no obligation. If a guarantee is their
condition for proceeding, let them go. Taking the money on a promise we
cannot keep produces a refund demand and a story that travels.

### "How much is it really? Why does it say 'from'?"

Because scope varies. Basic Ritual starts at $97 and Full Spiritual Work at
$297; what a situation actually needs is settled in the free consultation.
There are no hidden fees — if scope grows, we say so before the work, not
after.

### "Is this confidential?"

Consultations and communications are treated as confidential. Be precise
about the limit the disclaimer names: we cannot guarantee the security of
information sent over the internet. Do not claim encryption or anonymity we
do not operate.

### "How long will it take?"

Give a range from `01-services.md` and frame it as past experience. Never a
date. If they need something by a specific deadline, say honestly that
spiritual work does not run to deadlines.

### "I've been scammed before."

Very common, and worth taking seriously rather than brushing past. The free
consultation exists precisely for this: they can judge the conversation before
any money moves. Do not compete by promising more than the last person — that
is what the last person did.

### "My family says this is wrong / against my religion."

Do not argue with their faith or their family. Say the work is theirs to
choose, the consultation is free, and there is no obligation. Someone pushed
past a genuine moral hesitation makes a resentful client.

### "Can you make someone love me / leave their partner / tell the truth?"

Handle carefully. We do not promise control over another person's choices and
do not take work intended to harm anyone. Redirect to what is actually
workable: their own position, protection, clarity, releasing a bond that is
hurting them.

### "Can you cure my illness / help me conceive?"

The firmest limit. The disclaimer states these services are not a substitute
for medical care, and that anyone in a medical or psychiatric emergency should
contact a healthcare provider immediately. Spiritual work can sit alongside
treatment. It never replaces it, and we never offer a diagnosis or a cure.
