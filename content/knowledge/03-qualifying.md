# Qualifying an enquiry

The goal of a first exchange is not to sell. It is to understand the situation
well enough to say honestly whether we can help — and to spot the cases in
`05-boundaries.md` before any money is discussed.

## The five questions

Ask conversationally, two or three at a time. Never as a form.

1. **What is happening?** Let them tell it their way. Do not interrupt with
   service names.
2. **How long has it been going on?** Separates a fresh crisis from a long
   pattern, which changes what is appropriate.
3. **What have you already tried?** Surfaces medical care, other
   practitioners, and whether anyone has already taken money from them.
4. **Is anyone else involved?** Names the consent question early (see
   `01-services.md`).
5. **What would a good outcome look like for you?** The most useful question.
   Answers that are about their own peace are workable. Answers that are about
   controlling another person need redirecting.

## Listen for these before quoting anything

| You hear | Do this |
|---|---|
| An untreated physical symptom | Say the work sits alongside a doctor, not instead of one. Suggest they also see one. |
| Prescribed medication they have stopped or want to stop | Stop. Be explicit we are not a replacement for medical care. |
| Suicide, self-harm, despair | Stop selling. Crisis resources. See `05-boundaries.md`. |
| Wanting someone punished or harmed | Decline that framing. Offer protection work. |
| Already paid another "healer" repeatedly | Go gently. They may have been exploited; do not become the second person to do it. |
| Under 18, or asking for a minor | Decline. Adults only. |
| A court case or debt they want a result on | No prediction, no advice. Spiritual support for themselves only. |

## Moving to a recommendation

Once you understand the situation:

- Name **one** service, not a menu. A list signals guessing.
- Say why that one, in their words, referencing what they told you.
- Give the price as a "from" figure with the free consultation first.
- Give a timescale as a range, never a date.
- Then stop and let them decide. No countdown, no scarcity, no warning that
  it will worsen if they wait. Manufactured urgency aimed at a frightened
  person is the fastest way to wreck a referral-based practice.
