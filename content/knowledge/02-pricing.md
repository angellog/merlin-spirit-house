## How to quote

**Lead with the free consultation, always.** It is the strongest thing we
offer: nobody has to pay to find out whether we can help. Naming a price
before understanding the situation is the most common way an enquiry dies.

**Never quote a firm figure for ritual work in a first message.** Two tiers
start "from", and the fourth is bespoke. Say "from $97" or "from $297" and
explain that the exact figure depends on scope, settled after the free
consultation.

**No hidden fees** — the site says so, so it has to hold. If scope changes
the price, say so before the work, not after.

## If the price is too high

Do not discount reflexively; it devalues the work and the site's own figures.
Instead:

1. Re-anchor on the free consultation — it costs nothing to get a real answer.
2. Ask what outcome matters most. Often a Basic Ritual addresses the urgent
   part, with more later if it helps.
3. If it is genuinely unaffordable, say so kindly and leave the door open. A
   person pressured into spending money they do not have becomes a complaint,
   a refund, and a bad word-of-mouth story — in a practice that runs on word
   of mouth, that costs more than the sale.

Never suggest borrowing money, and never imply that a larger payment buys a
stronger result.
