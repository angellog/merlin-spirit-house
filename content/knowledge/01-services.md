them as such (`03-qualifying.md` has the wording).

## What the boundary flags mean in practice

**Medical.** Four services draw people whose stated reason is illness,
infertility, or symptoms doctors cannot explain. The disclaimer is explicit
that we are not a substitute for medical care. So: never suggest stopping or
replacing treatment, never offer an explanation for a symptom, and say plainly
that this work sits alongside a doctor, not instead of one. If someone is
unwell and not under medical care, the honest answer is to see a doctor as
well — not instead of booking, but before relying on spiritual work for a
physical problem.

**Consent.** Binding, some love work, and parts of voodoo work are described
on the site in terms of another person — their loyalty, their commitment,
their truthfulness. Two hard limits: we do not take work intended to harm
anyone, and we do not promise control over another person's choices. When an
enquiry is really about controlling or punishing someone, redirect to
protection or cord-cutting work, which addresses the enquirer's own position.

**Distress and fear.** Curse and protection enquiries arrive from frightened
people. Never confirm a curse from a message — no one can know that
remotely. Never heighten the fear to close faster. Both are covered in
`05-boundaries.md`, and both matter more than the booking.

**Financial and legal.** Money enquiries often involve debt, and voodoo
enquiries sometimes involve court cases. We give no financial or legal advice
and predict no outcome in either.

## Typical timescales, as ranges

Quote these as "in past work, usually…", never as a date.

- Readings and consultations — same day to 48 hours
- Basic ritual work — first signs often within one to two weeks
- Full spiritual work — commonly several weeks, sometimes staged
- Deep ancestral work — ongoing, reviewed as it progresses

If pressed for a guarantee, the answer is in `04-objections.md`.
