## What Is a Spiritual Attack?

A spiritual attack is the deliberate direction of harmful spiritual energy toward a specific person. The attacker uses rituals, invocations, or focused spiritual intention to send destructive energy into your spiritual field. This energy disrupts your spiritual infrastructure — the channels through which health, prosperity, love, and peace flow into your life — and creates openings through which misfortune, illness, conflict, and suffering enter.

Spiritual attacks originate from several sources. A jealous person — a rival in love, a competitor in business, an envious neighbor — hires a practitioner to attack you. A family member with a grudge performs spiritual work against you. A former friend or partner who feels wronged directs their anger toward you through spiritual means. In some cases, the attacker is someone you have never met who was hired by someone you know.

The attack manifests in your life as a cluster of negative developments that appear simultaneously or in rapid succession. Your health declines. Your relationships deteriorate. Your finances collapse. Your peace of mind evaporates. You feel a constant sense of dread, heaviness, and exhaustion that no physical explanation accounts for. These are not coincidences. They are the systematic destruction of your spiritual defenses and the opening of your life to harmful influences.

## The Signs of a Spiritual Attack

Recognizing a spiritual attack is the first step to defending against it. Here are the signs I see most frequently in my practice.

**Sudden, unexplained health problems.** You were healthy, and now you are not. Headaches, body pain, digestive problems, sleep disturbances, skin eruptions — symptoms that appear without cause and resist medical treatment. The spiritual attack is corrupting the flow of life energy through your body, and the physical symptoms are the manifestation of that corruption.

**Nightmares and sleep disturbances.** You experience terrifying dreams — being chased, being attacked, seeing dark figures, being paralyzed. You wake up exhausted even after a full night's sleep. You feel a presence in your room. These are signs that the spiritual attack is penetrating your spiritual field during sleep, when your defenses are naturally lower.

**Persistent bad luck across multiple areas of life.** Everything starts going wrong at once. Your car breaks down, you lose your job, your partner leaves, you get sick, a family member dies. This clustering of misfortune is not random. It is the signature of a spiritual attack that is systematically breaking down the channels of blessing in your life.

**Feeling constantly drained and heavy.** You have no energy. You feel a weight on your chest, a heaviness in your limbs, a fog in your mind. No amount of rest relieves it. This is the energy drain caused by the attack — the spiritual energy that sustains your vitality is being siphoned off by the hostile influence.

**Animals behaving strangely around you.** Pets become agitated or avoid you. Birds fly into your windows. Insects appear in unusual numbers in your space. Animals are spiritually sensitive, and they react to the negative energy that has attached to you. Their behavior is a warning sign.
